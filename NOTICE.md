This is an unofficial community plugin and is not affiliated with Monsters & Memories or Niche Worlds Cult.

Monsters & Memories names and game assets belong to their respective owners. Bundled spell and item icons were downloaded from the [Monsters & Memories Wiki Spell Icons](https://monstersandmemories.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Spell_Icons) and [Item Icons](https://monstersandmemories.miraheze.org/wiki/Category:Item_Icons) categories and their subcategories. Each selected wiki icon links to its file page. The plugin also includes original spell-themed symbols created for this project.

Wiki maps are shown from the [Monsters & Memories Wiki](https://monstersandmemories.miraheze.org/) under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); they are not bundled. The app shows each map's uploader and links to its file page.
