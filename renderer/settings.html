Each press of the key (or click on its frame) adds one. The count goes back to 0 once that many seconds pass without another press, and the bar under the number shows how long is left. For each press separately gives every press its own timer: the number is how many are still running, each running press stacks a tile behind it, and they run out one by one. The game doesn't say where your character is, so the minimap can switch to the zone you enter but can't follow you around it: use it, pan to where you are, and lock it again.

Map window

The tabs at its top switch between MnM Atlas and the Wiki maps. On a wiki map, drag to pan and scroll to zoom; when a zone has several, the one you pick is used for that zone from then on. Show on minimap puts the map you're looking at on the minimap, at the same spot.

Keys are watched passively, so the game still gets every key. Typing in chat also counts, so a "2" in a chat message starts the ability on 2. Use Reset all cooldowns and counters in the tray menu if that happens.

Frames are fixed places on your screen. If you move the game window, change its resolution or rearrange your hotbar, frame the buttons again. Only left clicks count.
